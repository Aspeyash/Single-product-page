Our Story

Crafting quality, one product at a time

We started as a two-person workshop in Old Dhaka in 2018, hand-finishing home goods that our neighbours couldn't find anywhere else. Six years later we still test every product ourselves before it goes up for sale.

Every item in our catalogue is sourced from local artisans we've worked with for years. We believe in fair pricing for both makers and buyers, and we personally respond to every question sent to our store chat — usually within the hour.
